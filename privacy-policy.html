Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Daisy Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy governs all mobile applications, games, and related services published by Daisy Game Studio on the Google Play Store and other mobile distribution platforms.

Summary for Players: Our games do NOT require personal user registration or passwords, do NOT request access to private contacts, sensitive GPS location, microphone, or camera, and do NOT sell personal user data.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy universally applies to all video games, puzzle titles, casual apps, and interactive entertainment software published under the Daisy Game Studio developer account. By installing and playing any of our games on your mobile device, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

We design our games with a privacy-first mindset, keeping data footprints minimal:

  • Local Gameplay Progress: High scores, level completions, virtual currency/achievements, and audio/graphics preferences are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as operating system version, device model, screen resolution, and language) may be processed by your operating system to ensure smooth rendering and stable performance.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers

To provide our games free of charge, our applications may integrate industry-standard third-party advertising networks, primarily Google AdMob.

These third-party providers may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) to serve contextual or interest-based ads, monitor ad delivery, and prevent fraudulent activity:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from apps
  • AdMob app-ads.txt: Our verified publisher identification is officially published at bobyjuniorse.github.io/app-ads.txt.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Daisy Game Studio is committed to the protection of children's privacy. For all our games:

  • We comply strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Advertisements in family-directed or casual puzzle titles are configured to request child-directed treatment and appropriate age-filtered ratings.

5. User Rights & Privacy Controls (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your country of residence, you maintain control over advertising identifiers and tracking preferences:

  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your Android system settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete Advertising ID.
  • Opting Out of Tracking: You can disable personalized advertising through your device settings or via in-game consent dialogs (where required by European regulations).

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect new game releases or updated platform requirements. All updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
