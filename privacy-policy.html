Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Daisy Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we do and do not collect, how third-party services operate within our applications, and your rights as a player of our mobile game Catdoku: Find The Cat Puzzle (Package: com.daisy.catdoku) and any related services.

Summary for Google Play Users: Catdoku does NOT require account registration, does NOT request access to personal contacts, precise GPS location, microphone, or camera, and does NOT sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number). The information processed falls into the following categories:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Your level progress, game settings (such as sound on/off), and puzzle states are stored strictly locally on your own device using local app storage.
  • Device & Diagnostics Information: Non-personally identifiable technical information (e.g., device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language preference) may be processed automatically by underlying operating system components for performance diagnostics and rendering.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers

To support free-to-play puzzle experiences, Catdoku integrates third-party advertising services, notably Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or IDFA) to serve contextual or personalized advertisements, measure ad performance, and combat fraudulent clicks, subject to Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from sites or apps
  • AdMob app-ads.txt: Our verified authorized publisher identifier is hosted officially at bobyjuniorse.github.io/app-ads.txt.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Catdoku is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We are dedicated to upholding the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Advertisements served are restricted to family-safe and appropriate content.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard all operations. Since our applications do not transmit personal user data to external proprietary databases, your gameplay remains self-contained on your device.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you hold specific rights regarding pseudonymous advertising IDs:

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized interest-based ads anytime via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.
  • Data Inquiries: You can contact us anytime regarding data privacy inquiries or requests.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect app feature additions or statutory adjustments. Any changes will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
